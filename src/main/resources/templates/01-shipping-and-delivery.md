# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## 1. Standard Delivery Estimate

EasyBuy's standard delivery estimate is **3–7 days** from order confirmation.

- This is an estimate, not a guarantee.
- Metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune) typically fall in the 3–5 day range.
- Non-metro, remote, or hilly areas may take closer to 5–7 days or longer.
- Delivery estimates for the specific order are shown in the order confirmation and tracking page — always direct customers there for order-specific information.
- The 3–7 day estimate is in calendar days unless the product listing or order confirmation states otherwise.

## 2. Tracking an Order

**Standard flow:**
1. Ask for the order ID or registered email to look up the order.
2. Retrieve the current status and tracking details from the backend.
3. Share the current shipment status, tracking number, and carrier name if available.
4. If tracking has not yet started (order just placed or being picked up), explain: "Your order has been confirmed and will be handed to the delivery partner soon. Tracking usually activates within 24–48 hours."

**Never invent:** tracking numbers, carrier names, locations, delivery dates, or status updates.

## 3. Order Has Not Arrived

If the expected delivery window has passed:

1. Check the latest order and shipment status in the backend.
2. If the order is still in transit: share the latest tracking update and explain that occasional delays can happen due to weather, holidays, or logistics.
3. If tracking has not updated in 48+ hours: escalate as a potentially stalled or lost shipment.
4. If the estimated delivery date has passed with no update: create a support ticket and escalate to the logistics investigation team.

**Say:** "I'm sorry for the delay! Your estimated delivery window was [X], and I can see your order is currently [status]. Let me escalate this to our team so they can investigate and get back to you within [escalation SLA]."

Do not promise a new delivery date or compensation unless the system provides one.

## 4. Order Marked "Delivered" but Not Received

This is a high-priority issue. Take it seriously.

1. Verify the delivery status in the backend — confirm it shows "Delivered."
2. Check for any proof-of-delivery notes or delivery photos if the system has them.
3. Ask the customer: "Could you check with anyone else at the address, or check if it was left at a neighbor's / security desk?"
4. If the customer confirms it was not received: escalate immediately as a missing delivery — do not dismiss this.
5. Create a support ticket with priority: HIGH, category: Shipping.

**Never say:** "The system shows it was delivered, so it must have been received." The delivered status is not proof of receipt.

## 5. Changing the Delivery Address

- Address changes are **only possible before dispatch**.
- Once an order is dispatched (status: "Shipped" or later), the address cannot be changed.
- If the order is not yet dispatched: check if the system supports an address change workflow; if yes, process it and confirm only after the system confirms success.
- If already dispatched: "Unfortunately, we can't change the address once the order is out for delivery. You may be able to coordinate with the courier directly once you receive the tracking link."

## 6. Failed Delivery Attempt

If a delivery attempt failed:
- The carrier typically makes 2–3 attempts before returning the item to the warehouse.
- Ask the customer to check the tracking page for any notes or contact instructions from the carrier.
- If the customer missed the delivery: "The delivery partner usually attempts re-delivery the next working day. You can also use the tracking link to reschedule or contact the carrier directly."
- If the item has been returned to origin (RTO): create a support ticket to arrange re-dispatch or initiate a refund.

## 7. Escalation Conditions

Escalate (create a HIGH priority ticket) when:
- Delivery estimate has passed with no tracking update in 48+ hours
- Order is marked delivered but customer reports non-receipt
- Shipment appears lost, returned, or stuck at a location for 3+ days
- Customer has already reached out about the same order before with no resolution
- A different address was delivered to

## 8. Quick Reference Answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How long does delivery take? | Estimated 3–7 days. Metro cities usually 3–5 days. |
| How do I track my order? | Use the tracking link in your order confirmation email or the "My Orders" section in the app. |
| My order is late — what do I do? | Check the tracking page first. If it's been more than 7 days with no update, contact support. |
