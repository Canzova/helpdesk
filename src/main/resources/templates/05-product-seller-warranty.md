# Product, Seller, and Warranty Support

## 1. Product Does Not Match the Listing

When a customer reports a mismatch:

1. Verify the order and item.
2. Compare the ordered product with the listing and available specifications.
3. Classify the issue as a possible wrong item, inaccurate listing, missing component, or product defect.
4. Check the applicable return or warranty policy.
5. Escalate if the listing and received product cannot be reconciled.

Do not invent product specifications or assert that a listing is accurate without checking it.

## 2. Seller Information

Seller ratings, reviews, listing completeness, product specifications, and displayed verification information may help customers evaluate a seller.

These indicators are not guarantees of product quality, authenticity, or future service.

Only describe information that is actually available in an approved data source.

## 3. Suspected Counterfeit Product

If a customer suspects a product is counterfeit:

1. Acknowledge the concern without confirming the allegation.
2. Verify the relevant order and product.
3. Collect evidence only through the approved workflow.
4. Route the case to the configured trust, safety, or product-investigation team.
5. Share only verified updates.

Do not declare the product genuine or counterfeit without a verified finding. Do not promise a particular investigation outcome or completion time.

## 4. Warranty Information

Warranty coverage is product-specific.

The customer or support agent should check:

- The product listing.
- The warranty document or terms supplied with the item.
- The manufacturer or seller warranty information, where available.
- The applicable claim process and eligibility result.

This knowledge base does not define a universal warranty duration, coverage scope, service center, or claim timeline.

## 5. Defects and Warranty Claims

When a product may be defective:

1. Identify the product and reported problem.
2. Check whether a return or warranty process applies.
3. Verify the warranty terms and claim eligibility.
4. Follow the approved claim workflow.
5. Escalate if the coverage or next step is unclear.

Do not assume that accidental damage, wear and tear, or every functional issue is covered or excluded. Use the actual terms.

## 6. Escalation Conditions

Escalate when:

- The delivered product does not match the listing.
- The product may be counterfeit.
- Product specifications conflict across sources.
- Warranty terms are missing or inconsistent.
- A defect requires technical assessment.
- The required investigation process is unavailable.

## 7. Unspecified Details

The following require confirmed policy or system data:

- Seller verification standards.
- Counterfeit investigation procedures and timelines.
- Warranty duration and coverage by product.
- Warranty exclusions.
- Manufacturer versus seller responsibility.
- Service center details.
- Warranty claim turnaround times.
