# Product, Seller, and Warranty Support

## 1. Product Does Not Match the Listing

When a customer receives a product that doesn't match what they ordered:

**Step-by-step:**
1. Apologize immediately — this is a high-frustration scenario.
2. Ask: "Can you tell me what's different about what you received?" (wrong model, wrong color, different brand, different product entirely?)
3. Verify the order and the product listed on the platform.
4. Classify the issue:
   - **Wrong item**: Different product than what was ordered → Return + re-ship or refund
   - **Inaccurate listing**: Product matches what was listed, but the listing was misleading → Report listing issue + return
   - **Missing accessories**: Product correct but accessories/parts missing → Partial return or seller contact
   - **Defective item**: Product correct but doesn't work → Replacement or warranty claim

5. If within 7 days: initiate a return/replacement request.
6. If after 7 days: check warranty options.

**Say:** "I'm really sorry about this — receiving the wrong item is absolutely not acceptable. Let me get this sorted for you right away."

## 2. Seller Information and Ratings

| Indicator | What It Tells You | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Seller Rating | Historical customer satisfaction | Doesn't guarantee future performance |
| Review Count | Popularity and experience | High volume doesn't mean every order will be perfect |
| "EasyBuy Verified" badge | Seller has passed identity and compliance checks | Not a quality guarantee for every product |
| Return Policy Shown | Seller's declared return terms | Must be checked per listing |

Seller ratings and badges are useful signals, not guarantees. A 4.8-star seller can still ship a defective product. Always rely on the order and product records when assessing a complaint.

## 3. Suspected Counterfeit Product

This is a serious issue — handle it sensitively and escalate promptly.

**Do:**
1. Acknowledge the concern without agreeing or dismissing: "Thank you for bringing this to our attention — we take product authenticity very seriously."
2. Ask the customer to describe why they suspect it's counterfeit (packaging, quality, serial number, price tag, etc.).
3. Ask for photos through the support ticket process.
4. Escalate to the Trust & Safety / Product Investigation team immediately.
5. Advise the customer: "Please stop using the product until we complete our investigation."
6. Keep the customer updated on progress.

**Never:**
- Say "This looks genuine to me" without an investigation.
- Dismiss the concern.
- Reveal investigation outcomes or seller actions before they're confirmed.

## 4. Warranty Information

Warranty terms are product-specific. There is no universal EasyBuy warranty — each product/brand/seller has its own terms.

**How to check warranty:**
- Check the product listing on EasyBuy (usually in "Product Details" or "Warranty" section)
- Check the physical warranty card or documentation in the product box
- For branded electronics: the manufacturer's website has warranty terms

**Common warranty types customers encounter:**
| Type | What It Covers | Duration (typical) |
|---|---|---|
| Manufacturer warranty | Manufacturing defects | 1 year (varies by brand/product) |
| Seller warranty | As stated in listing | Check listing |
| EasyBuy extended warranty | Accidental damage, extended coverage | Purchased separately; check order |

## 5. Filing a Warranty Claim

**Within 7 days of delivery (defective product):**
- Use the standard return/replacement process. Warranty process is secondary here.

**After 7 days (defect appeared later):**
1. Verify which warranty applies (manufacturer vs. seller).
2. Check the claim process: most manufacturer warranties require the customer to contact the brand's service center directly.
3. For EasyBuy-assisted claims (if the seller offers it): create a support ticket with the defect description, photos, and purchase date.
4. Share the nearest authorized service center information if available.

**What warranty typically does NOT cover:**
- Physical damage caused by the customer (drops, water damage)
- Damage from unauthorized repair
- Normal wear and tear
- Consumable parts (batteries after a year of use, etc.)

## 6. Missing Parts or Accessories

1. Ask what's missing: "Which accessory or part is not in the box?"
2. Compare with the product listing's "What's in the Box" section.
3. If a listed accessory is genuinely missing: treat as a wrong/incomplete delivery — initiate a return or escalate to the seller.
4. If the listing didn't mention the accessory: it may not have been included; check the product specification.

## 7. Escalation Conditions

Escalate when:
- Wrong product received and customer wants resolution (create ticket: HIGH priority)
- Suspected counterfeit — immediate escalation to Trust & Safety
- Warranty claim is disputed by seller
- Defect appears to be a safety hazard (electrical, chemical, etc.) — URGENT
- Product specifications conflict between listing and what was received
- Missing parts and seller is unresponsive
