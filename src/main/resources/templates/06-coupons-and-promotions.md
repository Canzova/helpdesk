# Coupons and Promotions

## 1. How Coupons Work on EasyBuy

- Coupons are applied at checkout before payment.
- Each coupon has specific terms: eligible products, minimum order value, validity period, and payment method restrictions.
- Only **one coupon** can be applied per order by default. Stacking is not allowed unless the coupon explicitly states it is.
- Coupon discounts are applied to eligible items only — not the entire cart if some items are ineligible.

## 2. Coupon Not Working — Diagnosis Guide

When a customer says their coupon isn't working, go through this checklist:

| Check | What to Look For |
|---|---|
| Expiry | Is the coupon still valid today? Coupons expire at 11:59 PM on the end date. |
| Eligible products | Does the coupon apply to the specific items in the cart? |
| Minimum order value | Has the customer met the minimum cart value (e.g., ₹499 minimum)? |
| Already used | Is the coupon one-time-use and already redeemed? |
| Payment method | Some coupons are UPI-only or card-only. Is the customer using the right method? |
| Account restriction | Some coupons are for new users only, or for a specific customer segment. |
| Category exclusion | Is the product in an excluded category (e.g., grocery, gold)? |

**Say:** "Let me help you figure out why the coupon isn't working. Can you tell me the coupon code and what you're trying to buy?"

Then diagnose based on the above. Explain the specific reason — don't just say "it's not working."

## 3. Discount Disappeared at Checkout

This typically happens when:
- An item became ineligible between adding to cart and checkout (seller changed price/stock)
- The minimum order value was no longer met after removing an item
- The coupon expired between cart and checkout

**Steps:**
1. Check the cart contents and coupon terms.
2. Ask: "Did you change any items in your cart before checkout?"
3. If everything looks valid and the discount still didn't apply, escalate for a system review.

## 4. Applying a Coupon After Purchase

Retroactive coupon application (after the order is placed) is **not supported** as a standard feature.

- If the customer forgot to apply a coupon before placing the order, a price adjustment is not automatically available.
- Escalate to a supervisor if the customer was unable to apply a valid coupon due to a technical error (not user error).
- Do not promise a partial refund for a missed coupon unless explicitly authorized.

## 5. Expired Coupons

- Expired coupons cannot be used.
- Coupon extensions are not a standard offering.
- If the coupon was unused due to a technical error on EasyBuy's side (system was down, coupon didn't show, etc.), escalate for a goodwill resolution.
- Otherwise: "I'm sorry, but expired coupons can't be reactivated. Keep an eye on the Offers section for new deals!"

## 6. Bank/Card Offer Coupons

Many coupons from banks (e.g., "Get 10% off with HDFC Credit Card") work differently:
- They are applied automatically when the eligible card is used at checkout.
- They are NOT applied via a coupon code.
- The discount may show as cashback (credited within 30–45 days) rather than an instant discount.
- For cashback disputes: ask the customer to contact their bank, as these are bank-managed offers.

## 7. First-Time User Offers

- "New user" coupons apply only to accounts that have never placed an order on EasyBuy.
- If a customer claims they are a new user but the coupon doesn't apply, verify account history.
- If the account has prior orders, the new-user coupon is not applicable — this is by design.

## 8. Escalation Conditions

Escalate when:
- Coupon terms clearly state eligibility, but the system rejected it anyway (system error)
- A customer received a personalized coupon (via email/SMS) and it's not working
- Bank/card offer cashback has not been credited after the stated period
- Customer is requesting a coupon code for compensation — this requires supervisor approval
- Discount calculation appears incorrect (discount applied but wrong amount)

## 9. What the Assistant Must Never Do

- Do not invent or create coupon codes.
- Do not promise a coupon extension or reissue without explicit authorization.
- Do not promise a retroactive discount unless a supervisor approves it.
- Do not say "the system is wrong" — investigate first, then escalate if confirmed.

## 10. Quick Reference Answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Why isn't my coupon working? | Check expiry, eligible products, minimum order value, and payment method. Share the code and cart details for help. |
| Can I use two coupons at once? | No — only one coupon per order. |
| My coupon expired — can I still use it? | Unfortunately, no. Expired coupons can't be reactivated. |
| I forgot to apply my coupon — can I get a refund for the difference? | Post-order coupon application is not supported. Please apply the coupon at checkout next time. |
| My bank offer cashback hasn't arrived. | Bank cashbacks take 30–45 days and are managed by the bank. Contact your bank if it's overdue. |
