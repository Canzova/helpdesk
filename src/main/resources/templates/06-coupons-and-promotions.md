# Coupons and Promotions

## 1. General Rule

Coupon eligibility depends on the specific coupon's terms and the application's verified response.

Do not assume that all coupons have the same requirements.

## 2. Coupon Is Not Working

When a customer reports a coupon failure:

1. Identify the coupon code or promotion.
2. Check its documented validity period.
3. Check the stated minimum purchase requirement, if any.
4. Check eligible products, categories, sellers, or customer groups, if the terms define them.
5. Check payment-method restrictions, if any.
6. Check whether the coupon has already been used or has other stated limitations.
7. Check whether combining it with another offer is allowed.
8. Use the application's verified rejection reason if available.

Explain only the conditions supported by the coupon terms or system response.

These checks are possible causes, not universal requirements for every coupon.

## 3. Discount Disappeared at Checkout

Check the promotion terms and the application's calculation or rejection message.

If the discount disappears despite the customer apparently meeting the stated conditions, escalate for review. Do not claim that the system is correct or incorrect without sufficient evidence.

## 4. Combining Coupons

Coupon stacking rules have not been defined in this mock policy.

Check the specific terms or the verified checkout result. Do not assume multiple coupons can be combined.

## 5. Expired Coupons

The policy does not define whether expired coupons can be extended, reissued, or replaced.

Do not promise an extension or issue a replacement coupon without explicit authorization.

## 6. Applying a Discount After Purchase

Retroactive discount adjustments have not been defined.

Do not promise a price adjustment or refund of the difference unless an approved policy and authorized system support it.

## 7. Escalation Conditions

Escalate when:

- The system rejects a coupon for a reason that conflicts with its documented terms.
- The promotion terms are missing or contradictory.
- A customer requests an exception or extension.
- A discount calculation appears incorrect.
- The applicable stacking or refund rule is unknown.

## 8. AI Restrictions

The assistant must not invent coupon codes, change expiry dates, modify eligibility, promise a discount, or claim a promotion has been applied without confirmation from an authorized system.
