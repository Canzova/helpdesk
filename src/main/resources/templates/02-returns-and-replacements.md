# Returns and Replacements Policy

## 1. Standard Return Window

EasyBuy's mock standard return window is **7 days**, subject to product category, item condition, and applicable product-specific terms.

The documentation does not define exactly when the return window starts or whether the seven days are calendar days or business days.

The assistant must not confirm eligibility based on the general window alone. Customer-specific eligibility should be checked through the backend whenever available.

## 2. Category-Specific Eligibility

Return conditions may differ by category.

### Garments

Check the product-specific terms and the condition of the garment. Tags, packaging, and signs of use may be relevant if the applicable policy says so.

Do not assume every garment is returnable.

### Footwear

Check the item-specific return terms and product condition.

Do not assume that worn footwear is eligible or ineligible without a documented rule.

### Innerwear

Hygiene-related restrictions may apply. The exact restricted items and conditions have not been defined.

Check the item-specific policy before confirming eligibility.

### Beauty and Personal-Care Products

Opened, sealed, or used condition may affect eligibility, depending on the product terms. The exact rules have not been defined.

Do not invent a universal rule for all beauty products.

### Electronics

Check the listing, product-specific return conditions, and warranty information.

A possible defect may require a return assessment or warranty process, depending on the applicable terms.

### Other Categories

Use the product-specific policy or authorized eligibility result. Do not assume all categories follow the same rules.

## 3. Requesting a Return

When a customer wants to return an item:

1. Verify the customer's authorization and order.
2. Identify the exact item and reason for return.
3. Check the order date, delivery information, and applicable return window.
4. Retrieve the item-specific conditions and eligibility result when available.
5. Explain the verified next steps.
6. Use the approved return workflow if supported.

Do not claim that a return request has been created unless the backend confirms it.

## 4. Damaged or Incorrect Product

For a damaged or incorrect item:

1. Verify the order and item.
2. Ask for a concise description of the problem.
3. Request photos or other evidence only through an approved process, when required.
4. Check the applicable reporting and return rules.
5. Route the case for assessment if eligibility or the remedy is unclear.

Do not guarantee a refund or replacement before the claim is assessed.

## 5. Missing Parts or Accessories

Ask the customer which part or accessory is missing. Compare the report with the product listing or included-items information when available.

If the included contents cannot be verified, escalate the issue rather than guessing what should have been included.

## 6. Products Outside the Return Window

If the standard seven-day window appears to have passed:

- Verify the relevant dates and the applicable start/counting rule if available.
- Check whether a separate documented process applies, such as a warranty claim for a defect.
- Explain that eligibility requires verification.

Do not invent exceptions or promise that a late return will be accepted.

## 7. Used, Opened, or Damaged Items

Eligibility depends on the category, reason, and item-specific terms.

A customer report of a defect should be assessed under the applicable return or warranty process. Do not automatically reject a defect claim solely because the product has been used.

## 8. Pickup and Replacement

Pickup availability, pickup fees, number of attempts, replacement eligibility, and replacement timelines have not been defined.

Confirm these details only through an approved policy or backend workflow.

## 9. Escalation Conditions

Escalate when:

- Category-specific rules are missing.
- The eligibility result conflicts with the written policy.
- The customer reports damage, a wrong item, or missing components.
- A request falls outside the standard window.
- The product's condition or warranty terms require specialist review.

## 10. Unspecified Details

The following need a confirmed policy:

- When the seven-day window starts.
- Calendar days versus business days.
- Exact non-returnable products.
- Tag, packaging, seal, and accessory requirements.
- Pickup fees and attempts.
- Replacement versus refund decision rules.
- Return shipping costs.
- Exceptions and approval authority.
